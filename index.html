    Hazards

    From dusk every vessel shows her navigation lights (Rules 20 to 31), and the USV identifies contacts by them.

    Simulator only: a simplified, transparent COLREGS model on a fictional chart. Not certified, not legally authoritative and not suitable for real-world autonomous navigation.

    Rules the engine applies, paraphrased from the IMO COLREGs (1972, as amended). Highlighted rules are active now.

    Classification: head-on when each vessel sees the other within 15° of her bow; overtaking when coming up from more than 22.5° abaft the beam. Risk of collision when the predicted CPA within the assessment window is under the safe distance. The USV is a power-driven vessel under 20 m.
